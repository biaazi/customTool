who to ask + what to say
Who to ask + when
close friend
Gut check / blind read
When you need a viewer not a critic. Show without context first. Their misread is data.
"before I tell you what this is — what does it feel like it's saying?"
design peer
Real critical feedback
When you need someone who understands the craft to tell you what's broken.
"what's not working? be mean if you have to"
peer / any friend
Stuck between two options
When you're looping. Binary ask only. No "both." Force a pick and a reason.
"pick one and tell me why — no both allowed"
toastmasters
Does this feel like us?
When you need the audience's read, not a designer's. They know the culture.
"does this match how the club actually feels when you're there?"
anyone close
No clear question yet
When you're stuck at a high level and don't even know what to ask. That's okay.
"I don't have a question yet — can I just talk through where I am?"
trusted peer
Pattern check
When you suspect something's off across your work, not just one piece.
"do you think my work has been less focused lately? I want the real answer"
Rules that held up
01
Send the messy version, not the cleaned-up one. The mess is the problem you need solved.
02
The fear is static, not signal. It's there every time. It's not telling you to wait.
03
The people you're most reluctant to ask usually have the most useful thing to say.
04
Give explicit permission to criticize. "Be mean if you have to" unlocks honesty.
05
You don't need a clean question. "I'm stuck" is a valid and complete ask.
06
Non-designers often give clearer feedback — they're responding as viewers, not makers.
